# Blinkit-Tableau-Project-
In today’s fast-paced retail environment, data-driven decision-making plays a vital role in enhancing customer experience and boosting operational efficiency. To put this into practice, I recently worked on a comprehensive Blinkit Product Analysis project, combining the power of Python for data exploration and Tableau for visual storytelling. 📊🐍📉

🎯 Project Objective:
To perform an in-depth analysis of Blinkit's product and outlet performance data to uncover insights that can guide strategic retail decisions.

🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used:
Python (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn): For data cleaning, preprocessing, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
Tableau: To design a dynamic and interactive dashboard.
EDA Techniques: Outlier detection, feature correlation, and distribution analysis.
Data Focus Areas: Item sales, outlet types, fat content, and outlet locations.

🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard:
1) Total Sales: $1.20M across 8,523 items

2) Outlet Size :

 🟡 Medium outlets: $508K 

 🟢 Small outlets: $445K 

 🟤 High outlets: $249K

3) Top-Selling Item Types: Leading item categories earned up to $178K each

4) Fat Content Insights:

 🥇 Low Fat: $776K

 🥈 Regular: $425K

5) Outlet Locations 

 🥇 Tier 3: $472K 

 🥈 Tier 2: $393K 

 🥉 Tier 1: $336K

6) Yearwise Trends: Steady sales since 2013, with a dip noted around 2010

📌 Business Takeaways:
✅ Medium and small outlets drive major sales — optimize resources here.
✅ High demand for low-fat products — an opportunity to expand the healthy food line.
✅ Strong potential in Tier 3 cities — invest in emerging markets.
